# 🎯 Recommended Go-To-Market Strategy

Your full playbook for launching the PCOS/PMDD planner business. Built for a
sub-$500 budget, no inventory, faceless, global. Everything referenced here is
already built and in this repo.

---

## The core idea

Don't sell "a planner." Build a **funnel + catalog** in a tight, growing niche:
**free tracker → email list → entry product → bundle → expansion products.**
Traffic is faceless organic content. Margins are ~95%. Risk is near zero.

---

## Phase 1 — Launch lean (Week 1)

**Goal: get ONE product live and learn.**

- Open your Etsy shop (brand: **Cycle & Bloom** or your pick).
- List **only the PCOS planner** first — one product, one clear message.
- Price **$12** (set list price $16, run an Etsy sale — Etsy favors discounts).
- Upload listing images `listing-1.png`…`listing-6.png`.
- Use the title/tags/description from `pcos-planner-launch-kit.md`.

> Why lean: launching all products at once splits your focus and your listing
> SEO. Nail one, then expand.

---

## Phase 2 — Build the funnel (Week 1–2)

**Goal: stop renting attention, start owning it.**

- Set up the **free 1-page tracker** + MailerLite landing page (`landing-page.html`).
- Turn on the **3-email welcome sequence** (`pcos-planner-lead-magnet-funnel.md`).
- Put two links in every bio: 🆓 **free tracker** + 🛒 **planner**.
- Start the **30-day faceless content calendar** (`pcos-planner-brand-and-calendar.md`).
  Post daily across TikTok + Reels + Pinterest. This is 90% of your effort.

> Why: a sale is one transaction; an email subscriber can be sold to forever.
> Every product you launch later goes out to this list for free.

---

## Phase 3 — Expand into a catalog (Week 3–4+)

**Goal: raise average order value without new traffic.**

Once you have a handful of sales and some list subscribers:

1. **Add the PMDD planner** as a 2nd listing ($12). Copy: `pmdd-and-bundle-listing-copy.md`. Images: `pmdd-listing-1…4.png`.
2. **Offer the Sage theme** as a free style choice inside the PCOS listing (or as a variant) — costs nothing, widens appeal.
3. **Launch the 3-in-1 Bundle** ($24, "save 30%"). This is your hero offer. Images: `bundle-listing-1/2.png`. Include `Bundle-Welcome-Start-Here.pdf`.
4. **Email your list** every time you launch something new.

## The pricing ladder (your money map)

| Offer | Price | Role | Profit/sale (~95%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free daily tracker | $0 | Builds the email list | — |
| PCOS planner (single) | $12 | Entry / impulse buy | ~$11 |
| PMDD planner (single) | $12 | Reaches a 2nd audience | ~$11 |
| Sage theme | variant | Widens appeal, $0 cost | — |
| **3-in-1 Bundle** ⭐ | **$24** | **Highest AOV — promote hardest** | ~$22 |

**Upsell logic:** most shoppers land on a $12 planner. The bundle at $24 doubles
your order value for buyers who want it all — at literally $0 extra cost to you.

## Marketing priority (where the wins are)

1. **TikTok + Reels (faceless)** — your main growth engine. Volume + consistency. One video can carry the shop. (Scripts: `pcos-planner-faceless-marketing-kit.md`.)
2. **Pinterest** — slow-burn evergreen. Pins drive Etsy sales for months. Pin every listing image with varied text overlays.
3. **Email** — compounding. Nurtures + relaunches to the same people.
4. **Etsy SEO** — your titles/tags are already optimized; reviews boost ranking, so ask happy buyers.

> Skip paid ads at this budget — they'll eat <$500 fast. Organic + email only
> until you're profitable.

---

## 90-day targets (realistic, not hype)

- **Days 1–30:** shop live, funnel running, 30 videos posted, first few sales + first ~50–200 email subscribers. *Treat this as learning, not income.*
- **Days 31–60:** PMDD + bundle live; double down on whatever content format got views; first consistent trickle of sales.
- **Days 61–90:** a content style that works + a growing list. This is where compounding starts. Reinvest time, not money.

*Most stores that fail quit before day 30. Consistency is the whole game.*

---

## Guardrails (don't skip)

- **Compliance:** every product = "tracking tool, not medical advice." Disclaimers are already baked in. Never claim treatment/cure. ✅
- **Validate** the niche live (10-min Etsy + Google Trends check) before pouring in time — steps in `START-HERE-launch-guide.md`.
- **Don't over-build.** You now have 3 products, a bundle, a funnel, and 30 days of content. **Ship it before building more.** Execution > more assets.
