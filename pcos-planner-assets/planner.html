HOW TO USE THIS PLANNER

Living with PCOS means a lot to keep track of. This planner gives you one calm place to track your cycle, symptoms, mood, energy, and everything you want to remember for your next appointment.

  • Print it, or import the PDF into a note app (GoodNotes, Notability)
  • Fill in a little each day — no pressure to be perfect
  • Use the monthly reflection to spot your own patterns
  • Bring the appointment-prep pages to your doctor visits

Take what helps, leave what doesn't. This is your space. 💗

Disclaimer: This planner is a personal organization and tracking tool only. It is not medical advice and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical guidance.
