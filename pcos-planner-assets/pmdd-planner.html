Welcome 🌙
HOW TO USE THIS PLANNER

PMDD symptoms tend to follow your cycle — often appearing in the days before your period and easing once it begins. Tracking daily, across at least two full cycles, helps you and your healthcare provider see your unique pattern clearly.

  • Fill in the daily tracker each evening — it only takes a minute
  • Use the monthly symptom chart to see your pattern at a glance
  • Compare two cycles side by side to spot consistency
  • Bring the appointment-prep pages to your visits

Be gentle with yourself. This is a tool for clarity, not judgment. 💜

Disclaimer: This planner is a personal organization and tracking tool only. It is not medical advice and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, including PMDD. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for diagnosis and care.

Tracking & Your Cycle
A QUICK ORIENTATION

Many people find it helpful to note which phase of the cycle they're in when logging symptoms. A simple way to mark it:

🩸 Menstrual — your perioddays ~1–5
🌱 Follicular — after perioddays ~6–13
✨ Ovulation — mid-cycledays ~14
🌙 Luteal — before next perioddays ~15–28

Cycle lengths vary — these are general ranges, not rules. Use the trackers to learn your rhythm.
