# Validation Runbook — find a winner before you build

Your last launch failed because it was **build → hope**. This is **prove → build**.
You have 20+ hrs/week and <$100. That's enough to run this loop 3–4 times until
something hits. Most ideas will flop in the test — that's the point. The test is
cheap; building the wrong product is expensive.

---

## Step 1 — Pick 3 narrow niche ideas (1 evening)

Rules:
- **Narrow beats broad.** Not "social media templates" → "content kit for *dog trainers*".
- **Sell to people who already spend money on their thing** (small business owners,
  coaches, trainers, real-estate agents, Etsy sellers, podcasters, Airbnb hosts).
- **Avoid the "faceless digital marketing / PLR reseller" trap** — that's people selling
  the dream to other beginners. Sell to real businesses instead.

Write down 3 candidates. Examples to spark ideas:
- Content kit (Canva templates + captions) for a specific profession
- Niche printable planner (e.g. ADHD-friendly, marathon training, meal-prep)
- A specific spreadsheet/tool (rental-property tracker, freelancer invoice + tax sheet)
- Niche clipart / PNG pack (only if you can design)

## Step 2 — Score each with FREE tools (1–2 hours)

Use the free tiers:
- **eRank** (erank.com) or **Everbee** (Chrome extension) on Etsy
- **Google Trends** (is the term growing or dying?)
- Plain Etsy/Gumroad search: how many listings already rank, and do top ones show
  lots of sales/reviews? Sales on competitors = proven demand (good). Zero competitors
  = often zero demand (bad), not a goldmine.

Score 1–5 on each, keep the highest total:

| Niche | Demand (sales on competitors) | Competition (LOW = good) | Can I make it well? | Total |
|-------|------------------------------|--------------------------|---------------------|-------|
|       |                              |                          |                     |       |

Target: a keyword with real buyer demand but **under ~5,000 competing listings**.

## Step 3 — Pre-sell it (this is the actual test)

1. Open `validation-lab/index.html`, edit the `CONFIG` block for your top niche
   (name, headline = the transformation, bullets, founder price).
2. Free email capture: make a form at **formspree.io** (free), paste the endpoint
   into `CONFIG.formEndpoint`.
3. Host free: drag the `validation-lab` folder onto **app.netlify.com/drop**.
4. You now have a live pre-sell page with a real "I want this" button.

## Step 4 — Send a little traffic (your 20 hrs go here)

Faceless, mostly free:
- **Pinterest**: 5–10 pins (mockup images) → link to the page. Evergreen, no face.
- **Reddit / Facebook groups** where that niche hangs out: be genuinely helpful,
  mention you're building X, ask if they'd want it. Don't spam.
- **One small ad test ($20)**: Pinterest or Meta, target the niche, send to the page.

## Step 5 — Read the signal, decide

- **Conversion = sign-ups ÷ visitors.** ~2–3%+ from cold traffic = real demand → BUILD IT.
- **Under ~1% after 200+ visitors** = weak. Change the headline/offer once and retest,
  or kill it and move to your next niche. **Killing it is a win** — you saved 40 hours.
- Bonus: email the sign-ups, ask "what's the #1 thing you'd want in this?" Their answers
  are your spec — now you build *exactly* what they asked for, and they're your first buyers.

## Step 6 — Only now, build

Once one idea passes, *then* we build the real product (I can help build templates,
tools, spreadsheets, pages). You'll be building something with a waitlist already
attached — the opposite of the dead Gumroad page.

---

### Honest expectations
- Expect 2–3 of your first ideas to fail the test. Normal. Cheap. Keep going.
- One validated product won't replace income; a **catalog of validated products** can.
- The work is real — it's marketing work, not a magic file. But with 20 hrs/week and
  this loop, you're now playing a game you can actually win, instead of buying lottery tickets.
