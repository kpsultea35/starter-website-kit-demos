**Purpose:** A free 1-page tracker to grow an email list of PCOS women, then upsell the full planner. Turns one-time scrollers into repeat buyers — fully faceless.
**Date:** 2026-06-21
**Cost:** $0 (Canva + a free email tool)

> Why this matters: ads/algorithms are rented audience. An email list is YOURS. A free tracker is the cheapest way to build it, and every subscriber can be sold the full planner + future products (PMDD, perimenopause).

## 2. Free email tool setup

Pick one free tier:
- **MailerLite** (free up to 1,000 subscribers — recommended, easy landing pages) ✅
- Beehiiv / Kit (ConvertKit) free tiers also work

Setup steps:
1. Create a free MailerLite account
2. Build a **free landing page** (their templates) — headline + email box + "Send me the free tracker"
3. Set the automation: on signup → email delivering the free PDF
4. Get the landing-page link → put it in your TikTok/IG bio (via Linktree/Stan)
