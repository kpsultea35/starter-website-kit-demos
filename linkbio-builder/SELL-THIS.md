# BioLink Builder — Faceless Seller Playbook

You now own a complete, sellable product. It's a **link-in-bio page builder** that
runs 100% in the browser. The buyer types their info, picks a theme, and clicks
**Export** to download a single `index.html` they host for free — no Linktree
subscription, no code, no monthly fee. That "own it forever vs. pay $5–25/mo"
angle is the entire sales pitch.

**Why this is a good faceless product**
- No backend = $0 running cost. You sell a file, not a service.
- Evergreen demand ("link in bio" isn't going away).
- Natural upsell to the website-kit buyers you already have on Gumroad.
- Nothing about it requires your face, voice, or name.

---

## 1. Package it (30 minutes)

Sell two things in one Gumroad product:
1. **The Builder** (`linkbio-builder/index.html`) — buyers open it locally and design their page.
2. **A "Quick Start" PDF** — 1 page: "Open index.html → design → Export → drag the file
   into Netlify Drop (netlify.com/drop) → you're live." Screenshots beat words.

Zip the folder, write a Gumroad description using the bullets above, price it at **$19**
(or $9 as an entry product / order-bump on your existing kit).

> ⚠️ Honest note: tools like this exist for free elsewhere. Your edge is **convenience +
> the bundle** (sell it *with* your website kit), not novelty. Don't claim it's unique;
> claim it saves them the subscription and the hassle. That's true and it sells.

## 2. List it where buyers already are (faceless channels)

- **Gumroad** — you already have a store; add it as a new product + order-bump.
- **Etsy** ("link in bio template", "linktree alternative") — huge faceless template market.
- **Payhip / Lemon Squeezy** — backups / wider reach.
- **Product marketplaces** (e.g. template directories) — list the free demo, link to paid.

## 3. Get traffic without a face

- **Pinterest**: pin screenshots of the 9 themes → link to the product. 100% faceless, evergreen.
- **SEO blog post**: "Free Linktree alternative you can self-host" on a simple site → link out.
- **Reddit/forums**: answer "linktree alternatives?" threads honestly with a link. Be useful, not spammy.
- **Short-form video**: screen recordings of the builder (no face/voice needed) on TikTok/Reels/Shorts.

## 4. Realistic expectations

- This is a **side income** product, not a "quit your job tomorrow" lottery ticket.
- Faceless digital products typically earn through **volume + a catalog**: one $19 product
  rarely changes your life; ten of them, cross-sold, can become real money.
- Your biggest lever is **distribution**, not the product. Budget your time accordingly:
  ~20% building, ~80% listing + traffic.
