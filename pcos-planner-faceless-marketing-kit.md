# PCOS Planner — Faceless Content & Marketing Kit

**Goal:** Drive free traffic to your Etsy PCOS planner using 100% faceless content (no face, no voice required).
**Platforms:** TikTok + Instagram Reels + Pinterest (all free)
**Date:** 2026-06-21

> Why faceless works here: your product IS the visual. Screen-recordings of the planner, calm B-roll, and text-on-screen out-perform talking-head videos for digital planners — and you stay anonymous.

---

## 1. Faceless content formats (what to actually film)

You only need a phone. No face, no talking. Pick from these repeatable formats:

| Format | What you show | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| **Planner flip-through** | Screen-record scrolling the PDF/Notion, calm music + text captions | ⭐ Easiest |
| **"Things I track with PCOS"** | Screen-record each page, text overlay naming the symptom | Easy |
| **Print + setup ASMR** | Hands only: printing pages, putting in binder, writing | Med |
| **Tablet writing** | Screen-record writing on the planner in GoodNotes | Easy |
| **Text-story / POV** | Aesthetic B-roll + text telling a relatable story → product | Easy |
| **Listicle / tips** | Text-on-screen tips ("5 things to track with PCOS") + planner clip | Easy |
| **Before/after organization** | Messy notes → clean planner | Med |

**Tools (all free):** CapCut (editing + captions + trending audio), Canva (text graphics), your phone screen recorder, GoodNotes/Notability (for tablet writing clips).

## 2. Ready-to-use video scripts (faceless)

Each = on-screen TEXT (hook → value → soft CTA). Pair with calm/trending audio. Keep videos 7–20 sec.

### Script 1 — The flip-through (highest converting)
- **Hook (0–2s):** `POV: you finally have ONE place to track your PCOS 🩷`
- **Body (2–12s):** *(screen-record scrolling pages, text labels appear)* `cycle tracker` → `symptom log` → `mood + energy` → `appointment prep` → `lab results`
- **CTA (12–15s):** `link in bio → digital download 🔗`

### Script 2 — Relatable pain point
- **Hook:** `things no one tells you about PCOS...`
- **Body:** `tracking symptoms in 5 different apps 😩` / `forgetting what to tell your doctor` / `losing track of your cycle` *(then cut to planner)* `so I made this 👇`
- **CTA:** `everything in one printable. link in bio`

### Script 3 — Doctor visit angle (your differentiator)
- **Hook:** `walk into your next PCOS appointment actually prepared 📋`
- **Body:** *(show appointment-prep + lab-results pages)* `track symptoms` / `write your questions` / `log your bloodwork`
- **CTA:** `PCOS planner → link in bio`

### Script 4 — Listicle
- **Hook:** `5 things to track if you have PCOS 🧵`
- **Body:** `1. your cycle` / `2. mood + energy` / `3. sleep` / `4. cravings + meals` / `5. symptoms before appointments` *(planner clip under each)*
- **CTA:** `I track it all in one planner → bio`

### Script 5 — Aesthetic ASMR (no talking, pure vibe)
- **Hook:** `printing my PCOS planner ✨` *(hands only)*
- **Body:** print → hole-punch → binder → write *(satisfying, calm audio)*
- **CTA (text card at end):** `get the digital download — link in bio`

### Script 6 — Trend-jack template
- Use whatever audio/format is trending that week; overlay `me organizing my PCOS life 🩷` + planner clips. Re-skin trending formats with your niche.

## 4. Posting strategy (free traffic engine)

- **Post 1–3x/day** across TikTok + Reels (repost same video to both + Pinterest)
- **Best times:** early morning, lunch, evening (test and check your analytics)
- **Consistency > perfection** — the algorithm rewards volume; expect some flops, one video can carry the shop
- **Batch:** film 7–10 clips in one session, schedule them out
- **Engage:** reply to every comment, especially "where?/link?" → pin a comment with the link
- **Pinterest bonus:** pin each planner mockup → links straight to Etsy, drives sales for months (evergreen)

## 7. Faceless growth rules
1. **Hook in first 2 seconds** — text + motion or people scroll past
2. **One idea per video** — don't cram
3. **Always end with a CTA** ("link in bio")
4. **Use trending audio** (CapCut/TikTok shows what's trending)
5. **Captions on screen** — most watch muted
6. **Post consistently for 30 days** before judging results
7. **Double down** on whatever format gets views; drop what doesn't
