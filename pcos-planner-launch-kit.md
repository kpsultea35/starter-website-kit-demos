## 1. Product Outline — every page/section to build

Build as a single PDF (Canva) AND/OR a Notion template. Recommended order:

### Cover & intro (2 pages)
1. **Cover page** — title, simple calming branding, year
2. **Welcome / How to use this planner** — 1 page, friendly tone + the medical disclaimer

### Core trackers (the heart of the product)
3. **Monthly cycle overview** — calendar grid to mark period days, cycle length, ovulation window notes
4. **Daily symptom tracker** — checkboxes/scales (1–5) for: mood, energy, bloating, cravings, acne, hair changes, headaches, sleep quality, pain
5. **Period & flow log** — start/end dates, flow level, cycle-length calculator row
6. **Mood & mental health log** — mood scale, anxiety/stress notes, journaling lines
7. **Sleep tracker** — hours, quality, bedtime/wake time
8. **Energy & fatigue log** — daily energy rating + notes on triggers

### Lifestyle / habit pages
9. **Meal & nutrition log** — meals, water intake, how foods made you feel (no diet claims — just logging)
10. **Movement / exercise log** — activity type, duration, how you felt after
11. **Supplement & medication tracker** — name, dose, time, taken ✓ (log only — no recommendations)
12. **Water & hydration tracker** — daily glasses checkboxes
13. **Habit tracker** — customizable monthly habit grid

### Reflection & medical-prep pages (high value — these differentiate you)
14. **Appointment prep page** — symptoms to discuss, questions for doctor, test results log
15. **Lab results tracker** — date, test name, result, notes (e.g., space for bloodwork)
16. **Monthly reflection** — what improved, what to discuss, goals for next month
17. **Symptom-pattern review** — quarterly overview to spot trends

### Bonus pages (boost perceived value / justify higher price)
18. **Goal-setting page**
19. **Self-care checklist**
20. **Notes / free journaling pages** (3–5 lined pages)

> **Tiers to sell:**
> - **Basic ($9):** pages 1–13
> - **Complete ($15):** all 20 pages
> - **Bundle ($25):** Complete + an undated version + Notion + printable formats

## 7. Differentiation tips (so you stand out)
- Lead with the **appointment-prep + lab-results pages** — most cheap planners skip these; they're the highest-value, most-shared features
- Offer **both printable AND digital (Notion/GoodNotes)** — doubles your audience
- Calming, specific branding ("for women with PCOS") beats generic wellness aesthetics
- Respond fast to messages + ask happy buyers for reviews (reviews = Etsy ranking)
