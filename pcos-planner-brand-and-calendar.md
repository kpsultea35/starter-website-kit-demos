## 2. Brand look (matches the planner assets)

- **Colors:** soft pink `#E8A6B8`, mauve `#B57B91`, cream `#FBF6F1`, sage `#9CB39A`
- **Fonts:** Poppins (headings) + Nunito/Lato (body) — all free on Canva
- **Emoji signature:** 🌸 (use consistently)
- **Tone:** warm, supportive, real, never preachy or clinical. Talk *with* the community, not at them.

## 5. Pinterest (the slow-burn winner — do this too)
- Make 5–10 Pins per planner mockup (different text overlays), all linking to Etsy.
- Pinterest traffic is evergreen — Pins keep driving sales for months, unlike TikTok.
- Boards: "PCOS Tips," "Cycle Tracking," "Wellness Planners," "PCOS Self-Care."

---

## 6. Expansion roadmap (after first sales)
1. **PMDD tracker** (reuse ~80% of the planner template)
2. **Perimenopause planner** (biggest market)
3. **Bundle** all three → highest AOV
4. Eventually: move to your own **Shopify store** once you're past ~$2k/mo to keep more margin + own the customer.
