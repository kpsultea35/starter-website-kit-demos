# PCOS Planner — Canva Build Guide (copy-paste content for every page)

**Purpose:** The exact text, headers, and labels for all 20 pages. Open Canva, create a US-Letter (8.5×11") doc, and paste each block onto its page. Duplicate to A4 + A5 when done.
**Date:** 2026-06-21

> Design defaults (keep it calm & on-brand):
> - **Fonts:** one rounded heading font (e.g. Poppins/Quicksand) + one clean body font (e.g. Lato/Nunito)
> - **Palette:** soft pinks/mauve + cream + sage green accents (calming, PCOS-community aesthetic)
> - **Layout:** generous white space, rounded boxes, light gridlines, checkboxes ☐
> - Add a small footer on every page: *"Not medical advice — for personal tracking only."*

## PAGE 2 — Welcome / How to use
```
Welcome 🌸

This planner gives you ONE calm place to track your cycle, symptoms,
mood, energy, and everything you want to remember for your next
appointment.

How to use it:
• Print it, or import the PDF into a note app (GoodNotes, Notability)
• Fill in a little each day — no pressure to be perfect
• Use the monthly reflection to spot your own patterns
• Bring the appointment-prep pages to your doctor visits

Take what helps, leave what doesn't. This is YOUR space. 💗

────────────────────────────────
Disclaimer: This planner is a personal organization and tracking tool
only. It is not medical advice and is not intended to diagnose, treat,
cure, or prevent any condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare
provider for medical guidance.
```
